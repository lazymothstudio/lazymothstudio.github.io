Foldway

A logic puzzle tower where every ladder unfolds once. Stand it up to climb or lay it across a gap, and it stays there for good. Where you put each one is the whole puzzle.

Get Foldway on Google Play

Simple pieces, real puzzles

  • 176 rooms across 15 worlds, every one checked by a solver and tested against a player who makes the obvious move.
  • New ideas as you climb: crates, gates, keys, clockwork sentries, ropes, floods, dark rooms and echoes of yourself.
  • A daily room for everyone, and the Endless Climb for when you want more.
  • Calm and offline: no timer, no account, hints when you need them, and options like relaxed sentries and a left-handed layout.
