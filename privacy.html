Foldway privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Foldway is a puzzle game made by Lazy Moth Studio. It does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking

Foldway has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app has no internet permission and cannot send anything anywhere by itself.

Music

The music is public domain (CC0) and plays from inside the app. Nothing is streamed.

Your progress stays with you

Finished rooms, stars, paper birds and settings are saved in the app's own storage on your phone.

  • If you have Android's backup turned on, Android may copy this to your own Google account so it comes back on a new phone. That copy is yours, and Foldway's makers never see it.
  • Uninstalling the app removes it from your phone.

Nothing is shared

Foldway has no share buttons and never sends your progress, rooms or anything else anywhere.

Children

Foldway collects nothing from anyone, including children.
